📊 Methodology

X-axis: Particle size in nanometers (logarithmic scale). Smaller particles absorb more efficiently, and in our testing data 60nm marks the practical ceiling where genuine nano-scale performance holds up.

Y-axis: Bioavailability percentage, meaning the share of each dose that actually reaches the bloodstream. A traditional oil tincture manages only 4-8%.

Our figures come from Certificates of Analysis, published pharmacokinetic research, and brand-supplied technical documentation that we verify before inclusion. Bubble size represents price per milligram. Last updated: May 2026.
